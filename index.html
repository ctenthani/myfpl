Event Info

Everything you need to know

About the Event

The BT42.195km Race is an annual long-distance event organised under the Malawi National Council of Sports. It features a full marathon, 10 km, and 5 km races, attracting elite athletes and community runners from across Malawi and beyond.

Key Dates
  • Registration opens: 18 August 2026 (target)
  • Packet pickup / Expo: 25–26 September 2026
  • Race Day: Sunday, 27 September 2026
  • Marathon minimum age: 20 years old on race day
What to Bring on Race Day
  • Your bib and timing chip (if issued)
  • Appropriate running kit and shoes
  • Personal hydration if preferred (stations will be available)
  • ID for collection if required
Medical & Safety

Professional medical cover, ambulances, and first-aid posts will be positioned along the course and at the finish. Participants with medical conditions should consult a doctor before racing and declare relevant information at registration.

Contact

Organising Committee Chair and MNCS contacts will be published here once the committee is fully constituted. For now, monitor official MNCS channels and this platform for updates.
