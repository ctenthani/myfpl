Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 August 2026 · Applies to the FPL Assistant website and the Android app published on Google Play.

1. Who we are

FPL Assistant (“the App”) is an independent Fantasy Premier League helper tool operated from Malawi. It is not affiliated with the Premier League, the FA, or Fantasy Premier League.

2. Information we collect

  • Team ID and email you enter for sign-in, plan activation, or Matchday digests.
  • Device storage (browser localStorage): saved squad, last Team ID, trial/plan session, display preferences.
  • Optional Matchday emails if you opt in (sent via our email provider, e.g. Resend).
  • Technical data typical of any website/app: IP address, browser type, and basic analytics if enabled by the host (e.g. Netlify).

We do not ask for your official FPL password. We do not store full payment card numbers; card/PayPal payments are handled by third parties (e.g. PayPal).

3. How we use information

  • To load and save your FPL team context in the App.
  • To unlock Pro/Ultra features after payment or trial.
  • To send Matchday deadline digests if you subscribe.
  • To operate, secure, and improve the App.

4. Data sources

Player, fixture, and live data are drawn from publicly available Fantasy Premier League API responses and related public information. Accuracy is not guaranteed.

5. Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We may use processors that host the site (e.g. Netlify), send email (e.g. Resend), or process payments (e.g. PayPal). Mobile-money payments use merchant instructions you provide and are confirmed off-app.

6. Android app (Google Play)

The Play Store package is a Trusted Web Activity (TWA) that displays the same website in a full-screen Android window. The same privacy practices apply. App signing is managed through Google Play App Signing.

7. Retention

Local device data remains until you clear site data or uninstall. Server-side email lists and activation records are kept only as needed to provide the service or as required by law.

8. Your choices

  • Clear browser/site data or uninstall the app to remove local storage.
  • Stop Matchday emails by signing in and turning off the option, or by contacting us.
  • Request deletion of activation/email records by contacting the operator.

9. Children

The App is aimed at a general audience of FPL managers. It is not directed at children under 13.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. The “Last updated” date will change when we do. Continued use means you accept the updated policy.

11. Contact

Questions about privacy or paid access: contact the operator via the channels published with the App (e.g. X/WhatsApp associated with the project, or the email used for Pro activation).
